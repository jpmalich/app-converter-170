## REGISTERED: RULING CCC, OPTION (b), STATED AS A MINIMUM (Howard, send-84)
"THE SHOULDER MUST SPAN AT LEAST THE INNER-TO-INNER GAP." The gap is
measured between the references the shoulder ACTUALLY TERMINATES AT.
A shoulder terminating at the OUTER strokes overspans that gap and
still passes — both drafting variants accommodated with no choice made
and no second case to detect. Third time a hedge word ("usually")
dissolved into a structural form; this one needed no inapplicability
path for inner-versus-outer.

Wired form (linework_read._ccc_joint / _ccc_end_ok):
- Joints are judged on RAW drawn lines (y clustered at line-weight,
  ink runs merged at line-weight) — NOT on gap_tol-merged strokes.
  SEND-82's "0.34/0.40 shortfall" was a MERGE ARTIFACT: the true
  shoulder's connector pieces (y=64.04: x[77.978,78.323] and
  x[79.389,79.769]) tie its rectangle to both members EXACTLY; the
  gap_tol merge had absorbed that run into a siding course line.
- Outside: an end may overspan its boundary stroke only within the
  existing joint law (gap_tol) — a joint, never a crossing.
- Inside: a shortfall must terminate ON the member's inner twin — a
  through-going vertical at the end (line-weight identity), with no
  other through-going vertical between it and the boundary stroke.
- The allowance comes from the drawing's own line separations. Purity
  pin holds: nothing tunes toward 54, 30, 58, 30'-2", 33, 2'-7", or
  any sealed figure.

## REGISTERED: THE INAPPLICABILITY GUARD — MEMBERS THEMSELVES
- Endpoint lands on NEITHER twin of a member → no joint.
- Member NOT double-drawn at the shortfall side (no through-stroke to
  land on) → no joint.
- Member carrying THREE OR MORE strokes (another through-stroke
  strictly between the boundary and the landing) → no joint.
NO SNAPPING TO THE NEAREST STROKE — inventing the nearest match is how
a tolerance sneaks back in through the endpoint instead of the span.
Consequence stated plainly: a 3-stroked member yields NO joint to its
outermost stroke; an inner double-drawn pair can still joint on its
own named references. The drawing names members only through what a
jog line terminates on.
